## Q1
**Format:** multiple_choice  
**Difficulty:** easy  
**Question:** Marco issued a check that was dishonored for insufficient funds. The check was presented 75 days after its date. What evidentiary presumption arises under BP Blg. 22?  
**A.** Knowledge of insufficiency is conclusively presumed  
**B.** Knowledge of insufficiency is prima facie presumed  
**C.** Knowledge of insufficiency cannot be presumed  
**D.** Only intent to defraud is presumed  
**Correct:** B  
**Explanation:** Presentment within 90 days creates prima facie evidence of the drawer's knowledge of insufficient funds. The presumption is not conclusive.  
**Source:** "The making, drawing and issuance of a check payment of which is refused by the drawee because of insufficient funds or credit with such bank, when presented within ninety (90) days"

## Q2
**Format:** multiple_choice  
**Difficulty:** easy  
**Question:** After receiving notice that his check was dishonored, Ramon paid the full amount to the holder on the fourth banking day. What is the effect under BP Blg. 22?  
**A.** He remains liable because payment must be made within three calendar days  
**B.** He remains liable because payment must be made before presentment  
**C.** He is not liable under BP Blg. 22 because payment was made within five banking days  
**D.** He is automatically liable but the penalty is reduced  
**Correct:** C  
**Explanation:** Payment or full arrangements within five banking days after notice constitutes a statutory defense. The period is measured in banking days.  
**Source:** "Such maker or drawer will not be liable if he pays the holder thereof the amount due thereon, or makes arrangements for payment in full"
