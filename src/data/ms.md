## Q1
**Format:** multiple_choice
**Difficulty:** easy
**Question:** Which item is classified as a manufacturing cost?
**A.** Selling commission
**B.** Direct materials
**C.** Office salary
**D.** Income tax expense
**Correct:** B
**Explanation:** Direct materials are one of the three manufacturing-cost classifications. Selling commissions and office salaries are period costs.
**Source:** "Manufacturing costs are classified as (1) direct materials, (2) direct labor, or (3) manufacturing overhead."

**B.** ₱195,000
**C.** ₱235,000
