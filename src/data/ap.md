## Q1
**Format:** multiple_choice
**Difficulty:** easy
**Question:** A customer confirms a balance lower than the client's ledger because an older invoice was already settled but the payment was posted to another invoice. What audit procedure should the auditor perform next?
**A.** A. Ignore the difference because the customer replied
**B.** B. Trace the customer's payment to the bank deposit and remittance details
**C.** C. Send a positive confirmation to the supplier
**D.** D. Recompute depreciation on the customer's equipment
**Correct:** B
**Explanation:** Tracing the payment and its posting identifies whether the customer's cash settlement was misapplied; merely accepting the confirmation does not resolve the ledger error.
**Source:** "Accounts receivable confirmation letters were sent to the customers. Customer B P36,000 Invoice dated 8/20 was already settled. Investigation revealed that payment was erroneously posted against account for an invoice dated 12/20 for the same amount."
