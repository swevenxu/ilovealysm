## Q1
**Format:** multiple_choice  
**Difficulty:** easy  
**Question:** Under PAS 7, which characteristic is required for an investment to qualify as a cash equivalent?  
**A.** It must have a long-term maturity and high yield.  
**B.** It must be short-term and highly liquid.  
**C.** It must be held for trading purposes.  
**D.** It must be convertible into equity securities.  
**Correct:** B  
**Explanation:** Cash equivalents are required to be short-term and highly liquid. The other choices describe characteristics not required by the PDF.  
**Source:** "cash equivalents are short-term and highly liquid investments that are readily convertible into cash"

## Q2
**Format:** multiple_choice  
**Difficulty:** easy  
**Question:** Which item is classified as a bank reconciling item?  
**A.** Credit memo  
**B.** Debit memo  
**C.** Deposit in transit  
**D.** Bank collection  
**Correct:** C  
**Explanation:** Deposits in transit are bank reconciling items because they affect the bank balance but have not yet appeared on the bank statement. Credit and debit memos are book reconciling items.  
**Source:** "Bank Reconciling Items - these include deposits in transit, outstanding checks and errors."

## Q3
**Format:** multiple_choice  
**Difficulty:** medium  
**Question:** A bank overdraft exists because a check issued by an entity exceeds its bank deposit. How is the overdraft generally classified?  
**A.** Current asset  
**B.** Noncurrent asset  
**C.** Current liability  
**D.** Contra-equity account  
**Correct:** C  
**Explanation:** The PDF states that a bank overdraft is generally a current liability. It is not normally offset against positive current bank accounts.  
**Source:** "As a rule, an overdraft shall be classified as a current liability and not offset against current accounts with a positive or debit balance."
